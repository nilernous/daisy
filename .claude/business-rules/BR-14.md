## Rule

The price of a variant is the product's effective price plus the variant's `ExtraPrice`. The effective price is `SalePrice` while the sale is active, otherwise `BasePrice`.

## Details

- A sale is active when `SalePrice` is set and the current time is within `SaleStartAt`..`SaleEndAt`.
- A missing `SaleStartAt` means the sale has already started; a missing `SaleEndAt` means it has no end. Both missing means a permanent sale price.
- `BasePrice` is at least 0; `SalePrice` is between 0 and `BasePrice`; `SaleStartAt` is before `SaleEndAt`.
- The effective price is always calculated by the backend; the client never sends prices.

## Notes

- The database does not prevent sale dates being set while `SalePrice` is empty; the application must reject this.
