## Rule

`OrderStatus` moves forward one step at a time and never backwards.

## Details

- `Pending` -> `Confirmed` -> `Shipping` -> `Completed`.
- `Pending` or `Confirmed` -> `Canceled` (BR-36).
- `Completed` and `Canceled` are final.
- Moving to `Completed` sets `CompletedAt`.
- A VietQR order (`PaymentMethod = Online`) can move from `Pending` to `Confirmed` only after it is `Paid`. An unpaid VietQR order therefore stays `Pending` until it is paid, canceled or expires (BR-41).

## Notes

- Only `CreatedAt`, `PaidAt`, `CompletedAt` and `CanceledAt` are stored; the times of `Confirmed` and `Shipping` are not recorded.
