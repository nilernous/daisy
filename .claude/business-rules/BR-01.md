# BR-01: Unique Email

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| ID | BR-01 |
| Category | Account & Access |
| Enforced by | Database (`UQ_Users_Email`) + Application |
| Source feature(s) | AUTH-01 |
| Used by | [UC-01](../use-cases/UC-01.md) |

## Rule

Each email address can be registered to at most one account. Email comparison is case-insensitive.

## Details

- The email is the login identifier and cannot be changed from the profile screen.
- Registration with an email that already exists is rejected.
