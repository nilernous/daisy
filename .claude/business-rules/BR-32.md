# BR-32: VietQR Payment Confirmation

## Rule

A VietQR order is marked `Paid` only when a verified transfer matches it.

## Details

- The QR code contains the receiving account, amount = `TotalAmount` and transfer content = `OrderCode`.
- A transfer matches an order when its content contains the `OrderCode` and the order is `Online`, `Unpaid` and not `Canceled`.
- The order is marked `Paid` only if the amount received is at least `TotalAmount`.
- On success: `PaymentStatus = Paid`, `PaidAt` = transfer time, `TransactionCode` = bank reference.
- Every verified transfer is recorded in `PaymentTransactions` (BR-42). Transfers that are underpaid, match no order, or arrive for an order that is no longer payable (already paid, canceled or expired) are left for staff to reconcile (UC-71).
- An unpaid VietQR order keeps its stock only until its payment deadline (BR-41).
