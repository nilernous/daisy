| Used by | [UC-34](../use-cases/UC-34.md), [UC-35](../use-cases/UC-35.md), [UC-57](../use-cases/UC-57.md) |

## Rule

An order is paid either by Cash on Delivery or by bank transfer via VietQR. Each order has exactly one payment.

## Details

- **COD**: the order starts as `Unpaid`; staff mark it `Paid` when the cash is collected.
- **VietQR** (`PaymentMethod = Online`): the order starts as `Unpaid` and becomes `Paid` when the transfer is confirmed (BR-32).
