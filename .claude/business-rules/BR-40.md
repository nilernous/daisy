## Rule

Dashboard figures are calculated as follows.

## Details

- **Revenue**: sum of `TotalAmount` of `Completed` orders, grouped by day or month of `CompletedAt`.
- **Order statistics**: number of orders per `OrderStatus`, for orders created in the selected period.
- **Top selling products**: total quantity of order items in non-canceled orders in the selected period, highest first.
- **New customers**: number of accounts with role `Customer`, grouped by day or month of `CreatedAt`.
