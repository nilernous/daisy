## Rule

Home page collections and sort options are defined as follows (active products only).

## Details

- **Featured**: `IsFeatured = 1`.
- **New Arrivals**: newest `CreatedAt` first.
- **Best Sellers**: highest `SoldCount` first.
- **Flash Sale**: products whose sale is active now (BR-14).
- **Sort by price**: uses the effective price (BR-14).
- **Sort by rating**: highest `AverageRating` first.
