# BR-12: Unique Catalog Identifiers

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| ID | BR-12 |
| Category | Product Catalog |
| Enforced by | Database (`UQ_Categories_Slug`, `UQ_Products_Slug`, `UQ_ProductVariants_Sku`, `UQ_ProductVariants_Option`) |
| Source feature(s) | ACAT-01, APRD-01, APRD-03 |
| Used by | [UC-43](../use-cases/UC-43.md), [UC-45](../use-cases/UC-45.md), [UC-47](../use-cases/UC-47.md) |

## Rule

Identifiers used in URLs and stock keeping must be unique.

## Details

- Category slugs are unique.
- Product slugs are unique.
- Variant SKUs are unique across all products.
- A product cannot have two variants with the same size and color.
