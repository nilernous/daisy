## Rule

A guest cart is identified by `SessionId`; a signed-in cart by `UserId`. A cart has at most one line per variant.

## Details

- Adding a variant that is already in the cart increases the quantity of the existing line.
- New lines are selected for checkout by default (`IsSelected = 1`).
