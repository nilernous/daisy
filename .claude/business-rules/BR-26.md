## Rule

A voucher can be applied only if all conditions below are met at the time it is applied and again when the order is placed.

## Details

- The voucher is active (`IsActive = 1`).
- The current time is between `StartAt` and `EndAt`.
- The order subtotal is at least `MinOrderAmount`.
- `UsageLimit` is empty or `UsedCount < UsageLimit`.
- At most one voucher is applied per order.

## Notes

- features.docx does not define a per-customer usage limit, so one customer can use the same voucher more than once.
