## Rule

The storefront shows only categories, products and variants whose `IsActive` is true.

## Details

- Hidden products cannot be added to the cart or wishlist.
- Existing cart lines for a hidden product or variant are shown as unavailable and cannot be checked out.
- Staff and Admin can still see hidden items in the admin panel.
