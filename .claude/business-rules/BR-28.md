| Used by | [UC-11](../use-cases/UC-11.md), [UC-32](../use-cases/UC-32.md), [UC-34](../use-cases/UC-34.md), [UC-35](../use-cases/UC-35.md), [UC-38](../use-cases/UC-38.md), [UC-45](../use-cases/UC-45.md), [UC-54](../use-cases/UC-54.md) |

## Rule

At checkout, the receiver details and the product details are copied into the order so later changes do not alter past orders.

## Details

- `Orders`: `ReceiverName`, `ReceiverPhone`, `ShippingAddress`.
- `OrderItems`: `ProductNameSnapshot`, `SizeSnapshot`, `ColorSnapshot`, `ImageUrlSnapshot`, `UnitPrice`.
- Editing or deleting an address, or editing a product, never changes existing orders.
