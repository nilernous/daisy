| Category | Inventory |

| Used by | [UC-15](../use-cases/UC-15.md), [UC-26](../use-cases/UC-26.md), [UC-31](../use-cases/UC-31.md), [UC-47](../use-cases/UC-47.md), [UC-51](../use-cases/UC-51.md), [UC-52](../use-cases/UC-52.md) |

## Rule

Stock is stored per variant in `StockQuantity` and can never be negative.

## Details

- A variant is in stock when `StockQuantity > 0`.
- A variant is low on stock when `StockQuantity <= LowStockThreshold` (default 5).
- Stock changes made by orders must be atomic so two orders cannot sell the same last item (BR-30).
