# UC-41: Write Review

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| ID | UC-41 |
| Feature | REV-01 |
| Group | Review & Rating |
| Actor(s) | Customer |
| Data | Reviews, OrderItems, Products |
| Business rules | [BR-08](../business-rules/BR-08.md), [BR-37](../business-rules/BR-37.md), [BR-38](../business-rules/BR-38.md) |

## Description

A customer rates (1-5 stars) and comments on a product they bought in a completed order.

## Preconditions

- The customer is signed in and has a `Completed` order.

## Main Flow

1. From the order detail, the customer chooses Review on an item.
2. The customer gives a rating from 1 to 5 and an optional comment.
3. The system checks that the order belongs to the customer, is `Completed`, and that the item has not been reviewed (BR-37).
4. The system saves the review as visible, taking the user and product from the order item.
5. The system recalculates the product's average rating and review count (BR-38).

## Alternative Flows

- **A1. Order not completed**: At step 3, the system rejects the review.
- **A2. Item already reviewed**: At step 3, the system rejects the review.
- **A3. Invalid rating or comment too long**: At step 2, the system shows an error.

## Postconditions

- The review is visible on the product page and counted in the rating.
