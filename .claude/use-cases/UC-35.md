## Description

A customer places an order and pays by bank transfer by scanning a VietQR code.

## Preconditions

- The customer is signed in, has selected cart lines and a shipping address (UC-32).

## Main Flow

1. The customer reviews the order, chooses VietQR bank transfer, and confirms.
2. The system validates and creates the order exactly as in UC-34 steps 2-4, with `PaymentMethod = Online`, `PaymentStatus = Unpaid` and a payment deadline `PaymentExpiresAt` (BR-41).
3. The system generates a VietQR code with the receiving account, amount = `TotalAmount` and transfer content = `OrderCode` (BR-32), and shows the payment deadline.
4. The customer scans the code with a banking app and completes the transfer.
5. The page checks the order's payment status periodically.
6. When the payment is confirmed (UC-36), the page shows that the order is paid.

## Alternative Flows

- **A1. Customer leaves the page**: At step 4 or 5, the customer can reopen the QR code from the order detail (UC-38) until the payment deadline.
- **A2. Transfer not received**: At step 5, the order stays `Unpaid`; the customer can cancel it (UC-40). If the deadline and grace period pass, the system cancels it and releases the stock (UC-72).
- **A3. Wrong amount or content**: At step 4, the transfer is recorded but not matched automatically; staff reconcile it (UC-71).

## Postconditions

- A `Pending` order exists; it becomes `Paid` once the transfer is confirmed.

## Related Use Cases

## Notes

- The payment deadline is feature CHK-08 (UC-72, BR-41): 30 minutes + 5 minutes grace by default.
