# UC-23: Add to Wishlist

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| ID | UC-23 |
| Feature | WISH-01 |
| Group | Wishlist |
| Actor(s) | Customer |
| Data | Wishlists |
| Business rules | [BR-08](../business-rules/BR-08.md), [BR-10](../business-rules/BR-10.md), [BR-18](../business-rules/BR-18.md) |

## Description

A customer saves a product to their wishlist.

## Preconditions

- The customer is signed in.
- The product is active.

## Main Flow

1. The customer chooses Add to wishlist on a product.
2. The system adds the product to the customer's wishlist.

## Alternative Flows

- **A1. Already in wishlist**: At step 2, nothing changes and the action is reported as successful (BR-18).
- **A2. Guest**: At step 1, the guest is asked to log in.

## Postconditions

- The product is in the customer's wishlist.
