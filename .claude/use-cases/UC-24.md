# UC-24: Remove from Wishlist

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| ID | UC-24 |
| Feature | WISH-02 |
| Group | Wishlist |
| Actor(s) | Customer |
| Data | Wishlists |
| Business rules | [BR-08](../business-rules/BR-08.md), [BR-18](../business-rules/BR-18.md) |

## Description

A customer removes a product from their wishlist.

## Preconditions

- The customer is signed in.

## Main Flow

1. The customer chooses Remove on a wishlist product.
2. The system removes the product from the customer's wishlist.

## Alternative Flows

- **A1. Not in wishlist**: At step 2, nothing changes.

## Postconditions

- The product is no longer in the wishlist.
