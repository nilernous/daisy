# UC-46: Show / Hide Product

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| ID | UC-46 |
| Feature | APRD-02 |
| Group | Product Management |
| Actor(s) | Admin, Staff |
| Data | Products |
| Business rules | [BR-07](../business-rules/BR-07.md), [BR-10](../business-rules/BR-10.md), [BR-11](../business-rules/BR-11.md) |

## Description

Staff hide a product instead of deleting it, to keep order history.

## Preconditions

- The user is signed in as Staff or Admin.

## Main Flow

1. The user toggles a product's visibility.
2. The system saves `IsActive`.
3. A hidden product disappears from the storefront; cart lines for it become unavailable (BR-10).

## Alternative Flows

- None.

## Postconditions

- The product is shown or hidden; orders are not affected (BR-11).
