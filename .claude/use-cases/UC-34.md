## Description

A customer places an order paid by Cash on Delivery.

## Preconditions

- The customer is signed in, has selected cart lines and a shipping address (UC-32).

## Main Flow

1. The customer reviews the selected items, address, voucher and totals, chooses COD, and confirms.
2. The system checks again that every selected item is active and in stock, and that the voucher is still eligible (BR-23, BR-26).
3. The system recalculates all amounts (BR-24).
4. In one transaction (BR-30), the system creates the order with a new `OrderCode` (BR-29), `OrderStatus = Pending`, `PaymentMethod = COD`, `PaymentStatus = Unpaid`, copies the address and item snapshots (BR-28), deducts stock, increases `SoldCount` and voucher `UsedCount`, and removes the ordered cart lines.
5. The system shows the order confirmation with the `OrderCode`.

## Alternative Flows

- **A1. Not enough stock**: At step 2 or 4, nothing is saved; the system shows which items are short.
- **A2. Voucher no longer valid**: At step 2 or 4, nothing is saved; the customer must remove the voucher or apply another.
- **A3. Price changed**: At step 3, if the total differs from what the customer saw, the system shows the new total and asks the customer to confirm again.

## Postconditions

- A `Pending`, `Unpaid` COD order exists; stock and counters are updated.
